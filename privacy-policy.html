Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Time Calculator (the "App", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the App. By using Time Calculator, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect the information that is necessary for the App to work. We do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, location data, or any tracking information.

1.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Your email address
  • Your password

Your password is handled securely by our authentication provider and is stored in a hashed form. We never have access to your password in plain text.

1.2 Content You Create

To provide the App's core features, we store the content you create, including:

  • Routines — their titles, colors, schedules, and recurrence settings
  • Tasks — their titles, descriptions, and durations
  • The relationships and ordering between your routines and tasks

1.3 Technical Information

A limited amount of technical data is processed so the App can function correctly, such as a unique account identifier and timestamps indicating when your data was created or last modified.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure
  • Store and sync your routines and tasks across your devices
  • Schedule notifications and alarms for your routines on your device
  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App's core functionality

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage and Synchronization

Your data is stored in two places:

  1. Locally on your device, so the App works offline.
  2. In the cloud, through our backend provider, so your data can be synced and restored across devices.

We use Supabase as our backend and authentication provider. Your account and content data are stored on Supabase's infrastructure and are subject to their security and privacy practices. Please review Supabase's own privacy policy for more details.

4. Notifications and Device Permissions

The App may request the following permissions to deliver its features:

  • Notifications — to remind you about your scheduled routines
  • Exact alarms — to trigger routine reminders at the correct time
  • Run at startup — to restore your scheduled reminders after your device restarts
  • Internet access — to sync your data with the cloud

All reminders and alarms are generated locally on your device. Notification content is not shared with us or any third party.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account remains active.

  • When you log out, your locally stored data is removed from the device.
  • When you log in on a new device (or as a different user), locally stored data belonging to a previous user is cleared.

If you would like to permanently delete your account and all associated data, please contact us at the address below.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Authentication is handled over secure, encrypted connections, and your password is never stored in plain text. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Effective date" at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
